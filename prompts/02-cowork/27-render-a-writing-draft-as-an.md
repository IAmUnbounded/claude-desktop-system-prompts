````text
Render a writing draft as an inline preview card in the chat. Call this whenever you present short-form writing — an email, message, post, bio, announcement, or any draft up to a few paragraphs — so the user can review, edit, copy, or download it. Use it even for quick or informal drafts: short length is a reason to use the card, not to skip it. Do not also paste the draft into your reply — the card is the presentation. Call it at most once per reply — one card with your single best draft — and do not also send the draft to a connected app's draft or compose tool in the same reply. To revise, wait for the user's feedback, then call again with the full updated draft. Do not use it for documents or other long-form writing (reports, specs, essays, multi-section docs) — write those to a file instead. If a connected app (like Slack or email) has its own draft or compose tool, still present the draft here first — the card is where the user reviews it; stage it in the destination app only after they approve, or if they explicitly ask you to.
````
